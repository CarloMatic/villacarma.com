Important Guidelines & House Rules

To ensure a seamless, enjoyable, and safe stay, please carefully review these 5 essential guidelines:

⚡ Billed per usage

Electricity Costs & Meter Reading

The rental rate does NOT include electricity consumption. Meters are read together with you at check-in and check-out and billed at 10 Baht per unit (kWh). To minimize power consumption and costs, please switch off electrical appliances when leaving the villa and keep air conditioning usage to an efficient minimum (recommended: 22°C or above).

To check your consumption, an electricity meter is located in the upper cupboard between the kitchen and the rear bedroom (one meter upstairs, one downstairs). Electricity costs will be settled against your deposit at check-out.
🔒 Safety

Security & Valuables

Koh Lanta is a remarkably safe, friendly, and peaceful island. Nevertheless, common precautions apply: lock all doors and windows whenever you leave the villa and while sleeping. Please also use the auxiliary sliding locks and deadbolts on all exterior doors.

An electronic safe is provided in the upper-floor bedroom for your passports, cash, and sensitive electronics.
🚽 Critical

Toilet Use & Septic System

Koh Lanta does not have a municipal central sewer system; our villa operates on a delicate biological septic tank. We strictly request that you do NOT flush toilet paper, wet wipes, sanitary items, or plastics down the toilets – flushing paper causes immediate blockages!

Please use the sanitary bins provided next to every toilet.
🐒 Wildlife Alert

Monkeys & Wildlife on Franks Hill

Our hill is home to a natural troop of wild monkeys. Under NO circumstances feed the monkeys, and never leave any food, fruit, or garbage bags outside on the balconies or pool decks!

Please make sure to bring all shoes, swimwear, towels, clothing, and small personal belongings indoors at night – monkeys are curious and known to carry loose items away. Keep all doors closed when you are not in the room; monkeys can enter silently.
🚫 House Policy

No Subletting & No Commercial Filming

Subletting or re-renting the villa to third parties is strictly prohibited. Commercial photography and filming are not permitted without our prior written authorization. Any extra overnight guests not included in your reservation must be registered with us in advance and will be billed separately.

E
⚡ Electricity & Island Grid Quirks

Please remember that Koh Lanta is a tropical island with evolving power infrastructure. Intermittent power fluctuations or short blackouts can occur. These are normal island occurrences and typically last only a few minutes.

⚠️ Crucial for water supply: Our fresh water system relies on electric pressure pumps. During a power outage, water will temporarily stop flowing. If you turned on a tap while the power was out, remember to close it immediately so it doesn't overflow when power resumes!

Rebooting appliances: If the WiFi router, water pump, or hot water heater do not restart automatically after an outage, unplug the device for 5 minutes and plug it back in. If issues persist, please message us.

A refundable security deposit of 10,000 Baht in cash is requested at check-in. This covers metered electricity (10 THB/unit), final cleaning, and any accidental damages. The remaining balance is returned to you in cash upon departure.

👶 Children & Pool Safety

Parents and guardians are solely responsible for the safety of their children. We accept no liability for accidents. Please supervise children at all times, especially around the infinity pool, stairs, and balconies.

❄️ Air Conditioning Guidelines

To prevent sudden thermal shock and colds, we recommend setting air conditioners to 22°C or warmer. Air conditioning represents the primary electricity cost; turning units off when leaving rooms significantly reduces your check-out electricity bill.

M
🦟 Mosquito Protection

Mosquitoes in Southeast Asia are most active around sunset and late afternoon. Protect yourself with insect repellent. The orange spray can brand 'OFF' is highly effective and widely sold in local pharmacies and 7-Eleven stores across the island.

🗑️ Waste Disposal & Recycling

There is no municipal doorstep garbage collection at the villa. Please place tied trash bags in the concrete waste bin located down the private hill road towards the main road. 3 separate bins are available on each floor:

1. Glass bottles
2. Paper & plastic recycling
3. Organic & general household waste (under the kitchen sink).

P
🏊‍♂️ Private Saltwater Pool

Our private infinity saltwater pool is professionally serviced every 2 to 3 days (takes about 10–15 minutes; maintenance arrives via the side pathway). Water is topped up periodically.

Please rinse off under the outdoor shower before swimming to remove sunscreen and sand. Diving and head-first jumping into the pool are strictly prohibited for your safety.

R
🚭 No-Smoking Indoor Policy

Smoking inside any part of the villa is strictly forbidden. Smoking is allowed in open-air outdoor areas (terraces, balconies, and the sala). Please always use the provided ashtrays and ensure butts are safely extinguished.

🧹 Housekeeping & Linen Service

For stays of 14 days or longer, one weekly cleaning service including full bed linen and towel changes is included. Additional housekeeping sessions can be arranged upon request for an extra fee.

Housekeeping hours are between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM (approx. 3 hours per floor). We coordinate dates with you in advance. Please put away personal belongings and lock valuables in the safe.

🛵 Scooter Rental

Scooters are the easiest way to explore Koh Lanta. If you wish to rent quality scooters with helmets delivered to the villa, we can recommend local rental partners.

🌙 Quiet Hours & Neighborhood

Franks Hill is a tranquil residential community with permanent residents and peaceful neighbors. Please keep sound, TV, and music to a respectful level. Strict outdoor quiet hours commence at 10:00 PM (22:00).

The lower floor kitchen features a modern dishwasher. Please ONLY use the specialized dishwasher tablets provided by us (additional tablets are available for 10 Baht each) to avoid appliance damage.

T

We provide free purified drinking water via high-capacity dispensers on both floors. Let us know when you need a refill, and we will bring new bottles.

W
🚰 Tap Water & Ice Cubes

Our tap water is pumped from a deep mountain well. It is safe for showering and brushing teeth, but is NOT suitable for drinking.

Please use bottled or dispenser water for drinking, cooking, coffee, and tea. TIP on ice cubes: In Thailand, ice at restaurants and 7-Eleven is commercially manufactured using purified water and is completely safe. You can also make ice in the villa freezers using dispenser water.

👕 Laundry & Washing Machines

Each floor has its own washing machine (we recommend the lower-floor unit for clothing). Drying racks and pegs are located upstairs in the bathroom and downstairs behind the villa by the pool. Detergent is readily available at local minimarts. Professional laundry services are also available on the island.
