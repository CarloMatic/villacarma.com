Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is the layout of Villa Carma structured?

The villa consists of two separate apartments (ground floor and upper floor) rented together as one large residence offering 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, 2 kitchens, and 2 living areas. This provides maximum privacy for groups and families.

Is the villa suitable for remote work & workations?

Yes. With 500 Mbps symmetrical fiber internet and complete coverage across all balconies, terraces, and directly at the pool, the villa is ideal for workations and seamless video calls.

How close is the villa to the beach?

Villa Carma is located on Franks Hill, approximately 300 meters (a 5-minute walk) from Pra Ae Beach (Long Beach).

How many guests can sleep at the villa?

Villa Carma comfortably accommodates up to 8 guests. The layout offers 4 spacious air-conditioned bedrooms with king-size beds, distributed across two fully independent floors.

Is there parking available at the property?

Yes, private free parking is available on-site for cars and motorbikes inside the property gates.

Are pets allowed in the villa?

To ensure an allergen-free environment for all guests, pets are generally not allowed. Please contact us for special inquiries.

How do check-in and check-out work?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM, and check-out is until 11:00 AM. We offer flexible self-check-in via a secure keybox, and our local guest manager is always available to assist.

What kitchen equipment is available?

The villa features two fully equipped kitchens (one on each floor) with stovetops, refrigerators, microwaves, toast ovens, blenders, and high-quality Italian espresso/coffee machines.

Are cleaning services and fresh linen included?

Yes, professional cleaning is provided before arrival. For longer stays, regular housekeeping, towel changes, and bed linen refreshes are included once a week.

Can you arrange airport transfers to the villa?

Yes, we can help arrange private van transfers directly from Krabi Airport (KBV) to the villa, including the car ferry transition. Please let us know your flight details in advance.
